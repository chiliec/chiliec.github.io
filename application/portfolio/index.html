TalkNative

Self-initiated · solo · work in progress

An on-device iOS text enhancer that helps non-native English speakers polish their writing — built on Apple Foundation Models (iOS 26), so nothing they write ever leaves their phone.

Impact

  • Private by design: zero servers, zero accounts — the entire enhancement pipeline runs on-device via Apple Intelligence.
  • Modular architecture: four independent Swift packages (EnhancerCore, PresetKit, HistoryKit, EnhancerUI), each with its own test suite.
  • Invoked from any app through a Share extension — meet users where they already write.

What I learned

  • Adopting a framework weeks after announcement, with WWDC sessions as the only documentation — no Stack Overflow safety net.
  • Scoping honestly: v1 ships with the Share extension; in-place text replacement is deferred to v1.1 rather than blocking release.

Indonesian Conversation Bot

Self-initiated · solo · live

A Telegram bot that helps English-speaking Bali expats practice Bahasa Indonesia through scenario role-play — a Claude-powered conversation partner that listens and talks back in text and voice.

Impact

  • Built while learning the language myself — every scenario (warung, gojek, immigration office) comes from my own life in Bali.
  • Full voice loop: Deepgram speech-to-text → Claude Sonnet as conversation partner → Google Cloud TTS speaking Indonesian back.
  • A second, lightweight model (Haiku) corrects grammar and pronunciation without breaking the conversation flow.
  • Monetized with Telegram Stars — real users paying real money.

What I learned

  • Designing for learners: corrections must encourage, not interrupt — tone is a product feature.
  • A two-model architecture balances cost, latency, and quality better than one big model doing everything.

ClaudeBar

Self-initiated · solo · shipped

A native macOS menu bar app that shows your Claude.ai usage at a glance — so you know how close you are to your limits before you hit them.

Impact

  • Shipped and publicly distributed through my own Homebrew tap (brew install chiliec/tap/claudebar).
  • Scratched my own itch, then found other users with the same one — open source on GitHub.
  • Small, native, fast: pure Swift, lives quietly in the menu bar.

What I learned

  • The full indie distribution path outside the App Store: signing, notarization, Homebrew tap, release automation.
  • A small scope you actually ship beats a big scope you don't.

Voice AI Agents at Axveer

Group project · work · my role: AI Engineer

Voice AI agents that answer business phone calls 24/7 and book appointments — telephony wired to LLM-driven conversation flows, grounded in each client's data with retrieval (RAG).

Impact (my role)

  • Designed and built the LLM conversation flows and the RAG backend that grounds agent answers in client data.
  • Built evaluation pipelines that measurably reduced hallucinations — reliability as an engineering discipline, not luck.
  • Businesses stop missing calls: the agent answers, books, and hands off gracefully when humans are needed.

What I learned

  • Live phone calls are the harshest LLM environment: latency budgets, interruptions, accents — every weakness is audible.
  • Evals before features: you can't improve what you don't measure.

Symplast

Group project · work · my role: Senior iOS/macOS Developer

A healthcare platform for plastic-surgery clinics in the US — patient messaging, video consultations, and clinic operations across iOS and macOS.

Impact (my role)

  • Led the migration of a 100k+ line Objective-C codebase to modern Swift — while the product kept shipping.
  • Cut the crash rate by ~40% through refactoring, monitoring, and defensive engineering.
  • Built secure messaging, video calls, and offline-first synchronization for clinical environments.
  • Mentored junior developers; drove architecture reviews (MVVM, Clean Architecture).

What I learned

  • How to evolve a large production system without stopping the ship — migration as a marathon of safe, boring steps.
  • Healthcare sets the reliability bar: a crash isn't an inconvenience, it's a clinic that can't see its patients.
  • Mentoring sharpened my own architectural judgment more than any solo work.
